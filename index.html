Located just 9 miles east of Downton LA, Martinsound Film Spaces offers 10 unique practical and standing sets with a nostalgic 60s-90s aesthetic. Deeply rooted in film history, this facility is a one-of-a-kind space for your one-of-a-kind film.

Film Spaces

Martinsound offers ten immersive retro film spaces, including our Abandoned 70s Apartment, NYC Metro Train, liminal 90s offices, vintage recording studios and soundstages, jail cell, Oval Office, creepy stockroom, and an 80s laboratory, all rentable by the hour.
