# Sample Website for Martinsound
This repository acts as a sample design for Martinsound's website, designed by <b>Reuven Reyman</b>.

<h2>Inspiration</h2>
Martinsound Film Spaces describes itself as a 70's / 80's aesthetic with nostalgic sets, grungy walls, florecent lights, and ancient technology lining the walls of some sets. As such, Reuven took inspiration from these eras when designing the layout and feel of the site. The main color scheme he pulled from a business card <b>Jacob Painter</b>, the studio manager, had designed for himself.

Currently, the site's only functioning pages are the <b>Home</b>, <b>Film Spaces</b>, and <b>Contact</b> pages. The other links redirect to Martinsound's existing website. Hopefully with just the three pages, the client can get enough of an idea of the "vision" to start investing into it.
